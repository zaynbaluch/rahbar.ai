# School Administration and Licensing Research

## Status

Research only. No administrator portal, organization hierarchy, pricing, payment, license grant, or revocation system is included.

## Questions to resolve

- What are the actual roles: teacher, headteacher, school operator, district operator, support staff?
- Which actions belong to each role?
- Can licenses be provisioned and revoked when devices are offline for long periods?
- What is the acceptable grace period for offline validation?
- How are lost, replaced, or shared devices handled?
- Which school records must remain available when a license expires?
- What support channel works in low-connectivity environments?
- Which pricing and payment model is operationally realistic?

## Design principle

Model the organization hierarchy and authority rules before designing screens. Avoid adding a second UI surface until the teacher app is stable and pilot requirements are validated.
