# Student Progress Research

## Status

Research only. The MVP stores confirmed grading sessions, but it does not provide longitudinal student profiles, encrypted handover packages, merge conflict resolution, or multi-device synchronization.

## Questions to resolve

- What is the smallest useful progress view for a teacher?
- Is a stable student identifier required, or are class-local names and roll numbers sufficient?
- What happens when names or roll numbers change?
- Which metrics are pedagogically meaningful and which could mislead teachers?
- What export format can a new teacher understand and verify without special software?
- How should duplicate imports and conflicting records be handled?
- What consent and safeguarding rules apply to student-level data?
- How long should records remain on a device, and how are they deleted?

## Recommended prototype

Start with a non-syncing, local-only design prototype and a sample export document. Validate it with teachers before adding production storage, encryption, import, or synchronization code.
